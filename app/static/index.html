Why Vector Caches Fail — Live Verification Lab

Bi-Encoder vs. Cross-Encoder NLI

Standard semantic caches (like GPTCache) use cosine similarity on dense embeddings. In production, opposite actions (e.g., enable vs disable) or swapped arguments share >0.85 similarity, causing severe false-positive cache hits. VeriRoute adds a Stage-2 NLI Cross-Encoder and hard invariant guard to eliminate them.
