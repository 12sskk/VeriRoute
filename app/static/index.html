Two-Stage Cache Verification

Standard vector caches (like GPTCache) use cosine similarity on embeddings. Opposing queries like enable CORS and disable CORS share over 80% similarity, causing false-positive cache hits. VeriRoute adds a second stage that verifies invariants (numbers, entities, negation polarity) and runs an NLI cross-encoder.
